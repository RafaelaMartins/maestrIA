Narrador:  In the epic adventure of a fantasy world, the setting is introduced as the realm of Cordelia, a medieval kingdom steeped in history and tradition. The climate is characterized by seasons of extreme weather, with snow-capped mountains towering majestically against stormy skies in winter, and lush green valleys and vibrant forests bustling with life during spring and summer.

The main character we meet is a seasoned warrior named Garland, once the finest knight of Cordelia who had gained great respect for his skill and strength. However, his own power started to corrupt him, turning him arrogant and prideful, challenging those who dared oppose him without heeding the consequences.

As our story unfolds, tensions rise within the kingdom due to Garland's increasing cruelty, leading to a growing sense of unrest and a desire for change among the people. Meanwhile, prophecies speak of four legendary heroes that will rise against the darkness threatening the realm, sparking hopes for a new era of peace and prosperity. The stage is set for an exciting RPG campaign where our heroic party can unite, face adversaries, and restore balance to this magical world.
